Monday keeps the scorebook open.

By Scoop · The Waiver Wire · October 8, 2026

Seven currently listed league starters play for Buffalo or the Rams. Their NFL game is scheduled for Monday at 8:15 p.m. ET, and several fantasy matchups will still have a player left to count.

Poorly Drafted lists James Cook and Davante Adams; its Week 5 opponent, Unnamed team, lists Josh Allen. The same late game could matter to both sides. LaPorta Potty’s four Thursday starters will already be in the books by the time Efficient & Ethical FB’s Kyren Williams and Dalton Kincaid reach Monday. Big Time Timmy Jim also lists Puka Nacua, after its London exposure on Sunday morning.

None of this is a comeback prediction. It is the shape of the weekend: one fantasy matchup can look settled because the teams have played different numbers of starters. Count the remaining players and check their availability before treating a Sunday margin as a result.
